Restoration services available throughout Wisconsin

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Wisconsin restoration crew

Restoration companies in Wisconsin are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Wisconsin property owners

Standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and fire, exclude flooding from rising water (which requires a separate flood policy) and gradual leaks, and often treat sewer backup and mold as optional or limited coverages. Frozen pipe claims may be denied if a home was left unheated, so check your policy's freeze provisions before winter travel.

Wisconsin restoration FAQs

When is damage most common in Wisconsin?

Winter and the spring thaw is the busiest period for restoration crews in Wisconsin. Wisconsin's hard winters freeze pipes and build ice dams, spring melt and intense summer rain flood basements, and many homes have aging sump systems that fail exactly when they are needed most.

Are the restoration companies in Wisconsin licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Wisconsin requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

How quickly do crews respond in Wisconsin?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Wisconsin may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Wisconsin?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Wisconsin are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.
