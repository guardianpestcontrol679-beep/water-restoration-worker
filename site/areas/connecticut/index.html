Restoration services available throughout Connecticut

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Connecticut restoration crew

Every crew we connect you with in Connecticut is a locally operated company. Expect a call back within minutes, an arrival window measured in hours for active emergencies, a moisture or damage assessment on arrival, a written scope and photo documentation that supports your claim. You are free to ask about licensing and certifications, and you should.

Insurance notes for Connecticut property owners

Coverage in Connecticut follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Document everything from the first hour, because prompt mitigation is a condition of most policies.

Connecticut restoration FAQs

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Connecticut?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Connecticut are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.

When is damage most common in Connecticut?

Mid-winter and the spring thaw is the busiest period for restoration crews in Connecticut. Connecticut's older housing stock, nor'easters and hard winter freezes make burst pipes, ice dams and wet basements routine problems, while coastal towns on Long Island Sound also face storm surge and wind-driven rain. Humid summers mean damp basements can turn into mold problems without dehumidification.

Are the restoration companies in Connecticut licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Connecticut requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.
