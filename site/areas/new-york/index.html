Restoration services available throughout New York

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a New York restoration crew

Every crew we connect you with in New York is a locally operated company. Expect a call back within minutes, an arrival window measured in hours for active emergencies, a moisture or damage assessment on arrival, a written scope and photo documentation that supports your claim. You are free to ask about licensing and certifications, and you should.

Insurance notes for New York property owners

Standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and fire, exclude flooding from rising water (which requires a separate flood policy) and gradual leaks, and often treat sewer backup and mold as optional or limited coverages. In hurricane-exposed areas, policies may carry separate wind or hurricane deductibles, so review your declarations page.

New York restoration FAQs

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in New York?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across New York are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.

Do you serve all of New York?

Coverage currently includes 363 communities and 674 ZIP codes across New York. If your town is not listed, call (888) 755-3821; service areas overlap and we can usually find a crew.

When is damage most common in New York?

Winter and the late-summer storm season is the busiest period for restoration crews in New York. New York ranges from dense pre-war apartment buildings in the city to lake-effect snow country upstate, so water damage comes from everything from burst risers and sewer backups to ice dams and roof collapse. Coastal storms and intense rainfall have repeatedly flooded basements across the metro area.

How quickly do crews respond in New York?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of New York may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.
