Restoration services available throughout Texas

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Texas restoration crew

Restoration companies in Texas are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Texas property owners

Coverage in Texas follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Because flash and river flooding are common here, a flood policy is worth considering even outside mapped flood zones.

Texas restoration FAQs

Do you serve all of Texas?

Coverage currently includes 605 communities and 1,355 ZIP codes across Texas. If your town is not listed, call (888) 755-3821; service areas overlap and we can usually find a crew.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Texas?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Texas are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.

Are the restoration companies in Texas licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Texas requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

How quickly do crews respond in Texas?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Texas may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.
