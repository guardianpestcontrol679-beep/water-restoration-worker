Restoration services available throughout Florida

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Florida restoration crew

Restoration companies in Florida are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Florida property owners

Standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and fire, exclude flooding from rising water (which requires a separate flood policy) and gradual leaks, and often treat sewer backup and mold as optional or limited coverages. In hurricane-exposed areas, policies may carry separate wind or hurricane deductibles, so review your declarations page.

Florida restoration FAQs

Are the restoration companies in Florida licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Florida requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

How quickly do crews respond in Florida?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Florida may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.

When is damage most common in Florida?

Hurricane season (June through November) is the busiest period for restoration crews in Florida. Florida's year-round humidity, intense afternoon storms and hurricane season create the most mold-friendly conditions in the country, and air-conditioning condensate leaks are one of the most common sources of interior water damage. Storm surge, roof damage and wind-driven rain during tropical systems account for many of the largest losses.
