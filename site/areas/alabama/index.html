Restoration services available throughout Alabama

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Alabama restoration crew

Restoration companies in Alabama are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Alabama property owners

Standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and fire, exclude flooding from rising water (which requires a separate flood policy) and gradual leaks, and often treat sewer backup and mold as optional or limited coverages. In hurricane-exposed areas, policies may carry separate wind or hurricane deductibles, so review your declarations page.

Alabama restoration FAQs

Are the restoration companies in Alabama licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Alabama requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Alabama?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Alabama are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.

Do you serve all of Alabama?

Coverage currently includes 374 communities and 529 ZIP codes across Alabama. If your town is not listed, call (888) 755-3821; service areas overlap and we can usually find a crew.

How quickly do crews respond in Alabama?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Alabama may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.
