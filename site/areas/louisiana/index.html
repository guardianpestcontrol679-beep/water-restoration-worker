Restoration services available throughout Louisiana

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Louisiana restoration crew

Restoration companies in Louisiana are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Louisiana property owners

Coverage in Louisiana follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Because flash and river flooding are common here, a flood policy is worth considering even outside mapped flood zones.

Louisiana restoration FAQs

How quickly do crews respond in Louisiana?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Louisiana may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.

Are the restoration companies in Louisiana licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Louisiana requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Louisiana?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Louisiana are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.
