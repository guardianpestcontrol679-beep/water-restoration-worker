Restoration services available throughout Massachusetts

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Massachusetts restoration crew

Restoration companies in Massachusetts are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Massachusetts property owners

Coverage in Massachusetts follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Document everything from the first hour, because prompt mitigation is a condition of most policies.

Massachusetts restoration FAQs

Do you serve all of Massachusetts?

Coverage currently includes 388 communities and 525 ZIP codes across Massachusetts. If your town is not listed, call (888) 755-3821; service areas overlap and we can usually find a crew.

When is damage most common in Massachusetts?

Winter and the early spring thaw is the busiest period for restoration crews in Massachusetts. Massachusetts has some of the oldest housing in the country, and nor'easters, hard freezes and ice dams repeatedly find the weak points in older roofs and plumbing. Finished basements across the suburbs are vulnerable to spring groundwater and failing sump pumps.

Are the restoration companies in Massachusetts licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Massachusetts requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

How quickly do crews respond in Massachusetts?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Massachusetts may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.
