Why fire damage gets worse by the day

Three forms of damage continue after the flames are out. Soot residue corrodes and stains surfaces progressively. Odor molecules migrate deeper into porous materials. And suppression water, often hundreds of gallons, swells, delaminates and eventually grows mold in everything it touched. Early stabilization changes what can be saved.

Emergency services on the first visit typically include board-up and tarping, water extraction, HEPA air filtration, protection of unaffected areas and an initial inventory of contents, with full cleaning and reconstruction following.

From board-up to rebuild

  1. Dispatch and assessment. A local crew confirms the property is safe to enter and plans the stabilization.
  2. Board-up, tarping and security. Windows, doors and roof openings are sealed against weather, animals and intruders.
  3. Water removal and drying. Suppression water is extracted and the structure dried to prevent secondary damage and mold.
  4. Soot and smoke removal. Surfaces are cleaned with dry sponges, HEPA vacuuming and the chemistry matched to the type of residue; ductwork is cleaned.
  5. Contents inventory, pack-out and cleaning. Salvageable belongings are documented, cleaned on or off site and stored; non-salvageable items are listed for the claim.
  6. Odor removal with thermal fogging, hydroxyl or ozone and sealing of structural surfaces, followed by full reconstruction.

What can be saved after a fire in Fall River

More than most people expect. Glass, ceramics, metal and sealed wood clean well. Many textiles are restorable with specialty laundering and ozone. Electronics that were not exposed to heat can often be cleaned and tested, and documents and photos can be freeze-dried. Items generally not salvageable include food and medicine exposed to heat or smoke, melted plastics, mattresses, particleboard furniture and heavily sooted drywall and insulation.

Fire Damage in Fall River: FAQs

Can I choose my own restoration contractor?

In nearly all cases, yes. Be cautious of anyone who shows up uninvited at a fire scene. Take the time to call a company you have vetted and get the scope in writing.

What about the water the fire department used?

Suppression water is removed and the structure dried as part of the restoration. Left in place it causes swelling, delamination and mold within days, compounding the fire damage.

Do you provide emergency board-up in Fall River?

Yes. Board-up, tarping and site security are dispatched around the clock so the property is protected from weather and intruders while the claim and cleanup get underway.

My neighbor had a fire and my place smells like smoke. Can you help?

Yes. Smoke that migrated through shared walls, windows or vents still leaves acidic residue and odor. Cleaning and deodorizing are covered by most policies as smoke damage, and local crews handle these losses regularly in Fall River.

Does my HVAC system need cleaning after a fire?

Almost always. Ducts, the air handler and filters collect soot and redistribute it every time the system runs. Cleaning the system and replacing filters is standard in smoke restoration.
