Common property damage problems in Vestaburg

Local conditions in Vestaburg follow the broader pattern across Pennsylvania. Pennsylvania's rolling terrain funnels heavy rain into flash floods, its rivers crest after spring snowmelt and tropical remnants, and its older homes and basements are prone to seepage, sewer backups and burst pipes in winter. Problems tend to peak during spring and the late-summer tropical remnant season, but plumbing failures and appliance leaks happen in every month of the year.

The calls crews receive most often from this area include:

  • Nor'easters that combine heavy rain or snow with wind-driven moisture and long power outages
  • Roof leaks around flashing, vents and chimneys
  • Flash flooding that pushes water through doors, window wells and foundation walls in minutes
  • Split supply lines that only start leaking once a hard freeze thaws
  • Frozen and burst pipes during winter cold snaps, especially lines in exterior walls, attics, garages and crawlspaces
  • Pinhole leaks in copper lines inside walls
  • Electrical fires from aging wiring, overloaded circuits and outdated panels
  • Space heater, fireplace and chimney fires during cold weather
  • Bathroom fans venting into cold attics
  • Window, door and siding leaks after heavy rain

Before the crew arrives

The first hour determines how much can be saved. While a crew is on the way:

  • Move rugs, furniture legs and valuables out of the water and off wet floors
  • After a fire, wait for the fire department to release the property and do not wipe soot with water, which smears it into surfaces
  • Open your insurance claim early; mitigation is a policy condition and delays can reduce what is paid
  • Shut off the main water valve if the source is a plumbing failure, and turn off power to the affected area if water is near outlets or appliances
  • If you see or smell mold, avoid disturbing it and keep the area closed off until it is assessed
  • Stay out of standing water that could be electrically charged or contaminated with sewage
