Restoration services available throughout Maine

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Maine restoration crew

Every crew we connect you with in Maine is a locally operated company. Expect a call back within minutes, an arrival window measured in hours for active emergencies, a moisture or damage assessment on arrival, a written scope and photo documentation that supports your claim. You are free to ask about licensing and certifications, and you should.

Insurance notes for Maine property owners

Coverage in Maine follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Document everything from the first hour, because prompt mitigation is a condition of most policies.

Maine restoration FAQs

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Maine?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Maine are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.

Are the restoration companies in Maine licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Maine requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

When is damage most common in Maine?
