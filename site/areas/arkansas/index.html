Restoration services available throughout Arkansas

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Arkansas restoration crew

Restoration companies in Arkansas are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Arkansas property owners

Coverage in Arkansas follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Because flash and river flooding are common here, a flood policy is worth considering even outside mapped flood zones.

Arkansas restoration FAQs

Do you serve all of Arkansas?

Coverage currently includes 171 communities and 228 ZIP codes across Arkansas. If your town is not listed, call (888) 755-3821; service areas overlap and we can usually find a crew.

Are the restoration companies in Arkansas licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Arkansas requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

When is damage most common in Arkansas?

Spring, when storms and river crests overlap is the busiest period for restoration crews in Arkansas. Arkansas sits in a corridor that sees heavy spring rain, river flooding along the Arkansas and Mississippi valleys and frequent tornado outbreaks. Humid summers slow natural drying, so wet building materials that are not professionally dried tend to develop mold quickly.

How quickly do crews respond in Arkansas?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Arkansas may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.
