Restoration services available throughout Washington

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Washington restoration crew

Restoration companies in Washington are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Washington property owners

Standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and fire, exclude flooding from rising water (which requires a separate flood policy) and gradual leaks, and often treat sewer backup and mold as optional or limited coverages. Frozen pipe claims may be denied if a home was left unheated, so check your policy's freeze provisions before winter travel.

Washington restoration FAQs

Are the restoration companies in Washington licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Washington requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.

How quickly do crews respond in Washington?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Washington may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Washington?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Washington are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.

When is damage most common in Washington?

The November-to-March rainy season is the busiest period for restoration crews in Washington. Smoke damage from regional fires is now a recurring summer issue.
