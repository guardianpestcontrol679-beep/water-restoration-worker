Restoration services available throughout Maryland

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Maryland restoration crew

Restoration companies in Maryland are independent local businesses. When you call (888) 755-3821, we route you to one that covers your ZIP code and has crews available. They assess the damage, stop further loss, give you a written scope and document everything for your insurance claim. Confirm licensing, insurance and IICRC certification before work begins, as you would with any contractor.

Insurance notes for Maryland property owners

Coverage in Maryland follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Document everything from the first hour, because prompt mitigation is a condition of most policies.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Maryland?

Are the restoration companies in Maryland licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Maryland requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.
