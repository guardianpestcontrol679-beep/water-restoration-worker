Restoration services available throughout California

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a California restoration crew

Every crew we connect you with in California is a locally operated company. Expect a call back within minutes, an arrival window measured in hours for active emergencies, a moisture or damage assessment on arrival, a written scope and photo documentation that supports your claim. You are free to ask about licensing and certifications, and you should.

Insurance notes for California property owners

Standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage and fire, exclude flooding from rising water (which requires a separate flood policy) and gradual leaks, and often treat sewer backup and mold as optional or limited coverages. Review your declarations page so you know what endorsements you carry before you need them.

California restoration FAQs

When is damage most common in California?

Wildfire season in late summer and fall, storm season in winter is the busiest period for restoration crews in California. California's property damage risks are unusually varied: wildfire and smoke damage in the wildland-urban interface, atmospheric-river storms that overwhelm roofs and drainage in winter, post-fire debris flows, and the everyday slab leaks and supply-line failures found in slab-on-grade housing. Older housing stock in coastal cities adds aging plumbing and wiring to the list.

Do you serve all of California?

Coverage currently includes 842 communities and 2,157 ZIP codes across California. If your town is not listed, call (888) 755-3821; service areas overlap and we can usually find a crew.

How quickly do crews respond in California?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of California may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in California?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across California are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.
