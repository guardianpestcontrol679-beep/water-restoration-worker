Restoration services available throughout Utah

  • Water damage restoration: extraction, structural drying, mold prevention and rebuild after burst pipes, appliance failures, roof leaks and flooding.
  • Fire damage restoration: emergency board-up, suppression water removal, soot and smoke cleaning, odor elimination and reconstruction.
  • Mold remediation: inspection, containment, HEPA filtration, safe removal and moisture correction under the IICRC S520 standard.
  • Sewage, storm and biohazard cleanup: contaminated-water protocols, disinfection and lawful disposal.

What to expect from a Utah restoration crew

Every crew we connect you with in Utah is a locally operated company. Expect a call back within minutes, an arrival window measured in hours for active emergencies, a moisture or damage assessment on arrival, a written scope and photo documentation that supports your claim. You are free to ask about licensing and certifications, and you should.

Insurance notes for Utah property owners

Coverage in Utah follows the same broad rules as elsewhere: sudden water discharge and fire are covered; floods, long-term leaks and neglect are not; sewer backup and mold are often sublimited or need endorsements. Because flash and river flooding are common here, a flood policy is worth considering even outside mapped flood zones.

Utah restoration FAQs

Do you serve all of Utah?

Coverage currently includes 130 communities and 220 ZIP codes across Utah. If your town is not listed, call (888) 755-3821; service areas overlap and we can usually find a crew.

Does Water Restoration handle commercial properties in Utah?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, multifamily and other commercial buildings across Utah are served by companies with commercial-scale crews and equipment.

How quickly do crews respond in Utah?

Active water and fire emergencies are typically scheduled for same-day response, often within hours. Response in rural parts of Utah may take longer because crews cover a wider radius, so call as early as possible.

Are the restoration companies in Utah licensed?

They are independent businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Ask for proof of liability insurance, any license Utah requires for the work (some states license mold remediators specifically) and IICRC certification.
