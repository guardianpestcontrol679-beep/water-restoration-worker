How it works

  1. Call (888) 755-3821. Tell us what happened and where the property is. Lines are answered 24 hours a day, every day.
  2. Get connected. We route you to a restoration company that covers your area and has crews available. They confirm details and give you an arrival window, usually within hours for active emergencies.
  3. Stabilize the damage. The crew extracts water, boards up openings, sets containment or whatever the loss requires, and walks you through a written scope before work proceeds.
  4. Dry, clean, remediate and rebuild. Work continues with daily monitoring and complete documentation for your insurance claim until the property is back to pre-loss condition.

Common questions

What does Water Restoration do?

We operate a 24/7 emergency line that connects property owners with local, independently owned restoration companies for water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold, sewage and biohazard cleanup. You call one number, (888) 755-3821, and we route you to a company that serves your ZIP code and has crews available.

Is there a charge to call?

No. Calling and discussing your situation is free and there is no obligation. You only pay for restoration work you agree to with the company that comes out, and covered work is usually billed directly to your insurance carrier.

How fast can someone come out?

Active water and fire emergencies are dispatched immediately and typically scheduled for same-day response, often within a few hours. Mold inspections and non-emergency work are usually scheduled within a day or two.

Do you work with insurance companies?

The restoration companies we connect you with deal with adjusters every day. They document the loss with photos, moisture readings and itemized estimates and, for covered mitigation work, generally bill the carrier directly.

Are the restoration companies licensed and certified?

They are independent local businesses responsible for their own licensing and insurance. We recommend asking the crew for proof of liability insurance, any state license required for the work, and IICRC certification, and getting the scope of work in writing before work begins.

Which areas do you cover?

Coverage currently includes 12,922 communities and 21,467 ZIP codes across 47 states. Browse the service areas directory or call; if your town is not listed, service areas overlap and we can usually still find a crew.
